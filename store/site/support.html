RetroSnake Classic Support

Play Classic Snake on the LCD of a 3D retro phone. Tap to begin, swipe to steer, collect food, and avoid collisions. The C button pauses the game; tap it again to open the phone menu, where you can change the level, maze, cover, sound, and display settings.

Frequently asked questions

Does it need internet? No. The game and its assets are included with the app.

Where are my scores? Scores and settings are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

How do I restart? Tap the phone's select key after Game Over. If your score qualifies, enter your initials first.

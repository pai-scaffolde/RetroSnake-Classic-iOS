Voxel Snake Support

Play Snake in a 3D voxel arena. Tap to begin, swipe to steer, collect food, and avoid collisions. Reach era portals during a run to travel from the monochrome grid through the present-day island to the neon future. Use the pause button to resume or end a run.

Frequently asked questions

Does it need internet? No. The game and its assets are included with the app.

Where are my scores? Scores and settings are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

How do I restart? Tap Play Again on the Game Over screen.

Contact

For help with the game, email pai@scaffolde.ai. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Voxel Snake Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Voxel Snake is an offline iPhone game. It does not require an account and has no ads, analytics, or tracking.

Information on your device

The game saves your scores, leaderboard initials, and game settings on your iPhone. That information stays in the app's local storage. The app does not send it to us or a third party.

Your choices

You can remove the saved information by deleting the app. There is no online account or server copy to delete.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit Voxel Snake Support.
